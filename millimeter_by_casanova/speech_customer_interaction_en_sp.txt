This allows us to apply natural stone in:
 – Ceilings
 – Walls
 – Backsplashes
 – Furniture
 – Drawers
 – Rounded legs and curved elements
Most importantly, it allows us to gain height, continuity and form — without excessive weight.
🟨 Pause / selection
 👉 Which of these applications catches your attention the most?

The key is that it’s not about choosing one or the other.
 In many projects, we combine both systems:
 – 2 cm where mass, structure or visual weight is needed
 – MILLIMETER where we want lightness, curves or special design freedom
🟨 Pause / feedback
 👉 Do you usually combine different materials or thicknesses in the same project?

We don’t work with stock.
 Each project is selected directly from the quarry.
The process is very simple:

There are no intermediaries — we go directly from quarry to fabricator.

The slab size depends on the block, but it’s usually around 120 by 76 inches.
